Refund Policy — Batch Docs

Effective date: October 31, 2026 Operator: Mikio Furusawa (globe Entertainment) ("we", "us") Contact: miv1982.biz@gmail.com

This policy applies to paid plans of Batch Docs purchased through Paddle, our merchant of record. It sits alongside the Terms of Service; if this policy and the Terms ever conflict on refunds, this policy describes how we actually handle requests.

1. Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep Pro access until then. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not by itself refund the current period.

2. When we refund

  • First purchase: if Batch Docs is not for you, email us within 14 days of your first purchase and we will refund it in full.
  • Renewals: if a renewal charge went through because you forgot to cancel, email us within 14 days of that charge and we will refund it.
  • Billing errors and duplicate charges are always refunded in full.
  • After those windows, refunds are at our discretion and are not guaranteed, except where applicable law or Paddle's own policies require one.

3. How to request a refund

Email miv1982.biz@gmail.com from the address used at checkout, and include your Paddle order number (it is on your email receipt). Please do not send card details — we never need them and Paddle never asks us for them.

Approved refunds are processed by Paddle back to your original payment method. How long the money takes to arrive depends on Paddle and your bank.

4. What happens after a refund

When a purchase is refunded, the associated license is revoked and the account returns to the Free plan at the end of the refund process. Documents you already generated are yours and are not affected.

5. Abuse

We may decline a refund where there is evidence of abuse of this policy (for example, repeated buy-and-refund cycles). This does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer law or Paddle's buyer terms.
